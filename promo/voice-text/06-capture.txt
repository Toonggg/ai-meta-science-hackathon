You install two hooks: one in Claude Code, and one in git. From then on, every instruction, command, result and commit is captured automatically, into a raw log on your own machine. Episteme groups that stream into episodes, and turns each one into a short diary of decisions: plans, runs, surprises, and changes of approach. The raw log keeps everything. The diary keeps only what a reviewer needs, and every diary entry points back to the raw evidence behind it.
