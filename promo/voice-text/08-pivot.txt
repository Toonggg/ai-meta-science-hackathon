Step four is the change of approach. Episteme's rules flag it, and ask for the reason, in the researcher's own words. If a language model is connected, it proposes the reason, but only as a word-for-word quote of what was said at the time. Anything it can't quote is marked as inferred, and never presented as fact. The researcher confirms it with one click. Now the reason is on the record, linked to the surprise that triggered it.
