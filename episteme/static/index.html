Your studies

    Load a demo

    A simulated study captured as a real session would be, with a change of approach at step 4. Ask the log why. A demo you loaded before opens where you left it; delete it from your studies to start it fresh.

    Research diary

    Plans, runs, surprises and pivots, with the why. The badge on each entry says who stated it: the researcher, the agent, or Episteme's own inference (lower trust).

      Model provider
      
    
      Episteme works offline with heuristics. Connect any model for deeper analysis. Settings and keys stay on this machine in .episteme/config.json.
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Search sources
      
      
      Free sources are queried together and merged. If one is down or rate-limited it is paused and the others cover for it. Web engines need your own key and are tried in order until one answers. All sources are re-checked every 10 minutes.
