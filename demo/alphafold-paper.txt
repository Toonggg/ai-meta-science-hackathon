Abstract
We demonstrate that end-to-end structure prediction using Evoformer and Invariant Point Attention substantially outperforms 2-stage physical relaxation baselines on challenging CASP14 targets (GDT_TS 89.4 vs 61.2, Table 1). This proves that direct coordinate refinement is necessary for atomic accuracy. Our approach generalizes across all proteins and orphan targets. These results suggest that deep learning models could accelerate structural biology.

1 Introduction
Accurate protein 3D structure prediction from sequence alone is a grand challenge in structural biology. Prior distogram methods stall around GDT 60.

3 Results
Table 1 reports evaluation metrics on CASP14 target T1024. The ResNet distogram baseline with Rosetta relaxation reaches a GDT_TS of 61.2. The end-to-end Evoformer architecture achieves a GDT_TS of 89.4 and average pLDDT of 92.1 (single run, seed 42). Figure 2 shows that invariant point attention eliminates steric clashes. The end-to-end model is also faster to train than the baseline.

4 Discussion
We find that iterative recycling explains most of the improvement (Figure 2). The model is robust to sequence depth.
